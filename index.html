01 Introduction

Curiosity that wants to understand intelligence.

I am a Grade 9 student drawn to the questions beneath modern technology: how machines learn from data, how they recognize images and language, and how creative systems can open new ways of thinking.

My curiosity is matched by a strong creative instinct. Programming is how I test ideas, deep learning is how I explore patterns, and every new concept is an invitation to build something more thoughtful. This portfolio is a growing record of that journey.
