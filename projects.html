01 Featured Project

SmartGym AI

Intelligent Gym Management & Churn Prediction

01 Results Reported

A machine-learning and deep-learning project designed to analyze gym member information, estimate the probability of customer churn, identify members who may be at risk of not renewing, and present useful insights through data visualization.

Dataset
4,000 records
Core model
Custom CNN with TensorFlow / Keras
Comparison model
Random Forest
View Performance Results

A

Overview

SmartGym AI analyzes gym member information to estimate each member's probability of churn and identify those who may be at risk of not renewing. The project combines data preprocessing, feature engineering, a custom convolutional neural network, a Random Forest comparison model, threshold tuning, and data visualization, and presents its results through a prediction engine and clear visual summaries.

This system has not been deployed in a real gym. The results come from the current project evaluation and have not been independently validated.

B

Problem Statement

Membership renewal is central to a gym's stability, yet the early signs that a member may leave are often scattered across member information and noticed too late. The challenge is to convert that information into a churn probability that can be reviewed, ranked, and explained, so that members at risk can be identified sooner.

C

Project Objectives

Objective 01

Analyze gym member information and prepare it for modelling.

Objective 02

Estimate the probability of churn for each member.

Objective 03

Classify members into risk levels to highlight those who may need attention.

Objective 04

Compare a custom CNN with a Random Forest comparison model.

Objective 05

Tune the decision threshold and evaluate performance with several metrics.

Objective 06

Present insights through clear data visualizations.

Model Development

The project was developed through the following stages.

  1. Stage 01

    Data Preprocessing

    Preparing 4,000 member records for modelling.

  2. Stage 02

    Feature Engineering

    Creating model inputs from the available member information.

  3. Stage 03

G

Key Learning Outcomes

Lesson 01

Preprocessing and feature engineering shape what a model can learn.

Lesson 02

A deep-learning model should be measured against a simpler baseline such as Random Forest.

Lesson 03

A probability becomes actionable only once a decision threshold is chosen, and that choice changes precision and recall.

Lesson 04

Accuracy alone is not enough: each metric reveals a different aspect of performance.

Lesson 05

Clear visualization makes model output easier to interpret for non-specialist readers.

03 Process

Behind the Projects

Good projects share a working rhythm, even when the tools differ.

Step 01

Understanding the Problem

Define the question, the context, and what a useful result would look like before choosing tools or models.

Step 02

Exploring & Preparing Data

Inspect the records, resolve inconsistencies, and engineer features that make patterns easier to learn.

Step 03

Developing & Evaluating Models

Build candidate models and measure them with several metrics rather than a single number.

Step 04

Learning from Experiments

Treat every result as evidence: record what worked, what failed, and what it suggests next.

Step 05

Improving through Iteration

Refine features, thresholds, and models in cycles until the solution is clearer and more reliable.

These stages do not apply identically to every project. SmartGym AI relies heavily on data preparation and model evaluation, while the ethics game centers on research, educational design, and teamwork rather than model training.
